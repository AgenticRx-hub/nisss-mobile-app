# Delivery plan

Agile delivery in two-week sprints (RFB §2.2.1): sprint planning, a demonstration to NISSS at the end of every sprint, sprint review reports and release notes, continuous integration and testing.

| Milestone | Scope (RFB Implementation Schedule) | Delivery | Acceptance | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | User account management, dashboard, transaction monitoring, payments and receipts, pension estimator (v1) | Week 10 | Week 12 | 22% |
| 2 | Notifications and alerts, customer support, information services, content management; Phase 1 close-out | Week 24 | Week 26 | 33.5% |
| 3 | Core user information services | Week 44 | Week 46 | 11% |
| 4 | Communication and notification services | Week 54 | Week 56 | 11% |
| 5 | Claims and benefits digitisation; Phase 2 close-out | Week 68 | Week 70 | 22.5% |

Weeks 1–4 cover mobilisation, requirements validation (business process diagrams, use cases, Requirements Validation Report) and architecture and security design. NISSS approval of designs is obtained before development of each module (RFB §4.1.1).

Warranty: 12 months from final acceptance. Post-warranty maintenance: 24 months, renewable.
